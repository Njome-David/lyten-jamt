## 1. Demo Video Recording & Upload Checklist

- [ ] **Recording Setup**:
  - Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum, 60 FPS recommended.
  - Audio: Sound effects and cosmic drone ambience clearly audible (verify mute is OFF; audio activates on first user click).
  - Browser: Fullscreen (press `F11`) with no bookmarks bar, extensions, or developer console visible.
- [ ] **Content & Child Safety Rules (Strict)**:
  - [ ] **NO face, likeness, or voice of any minor (under 18)** appears in the video (strict AGENTS.md rule 4.11 & NASA Space Apps policy).
  - [ ] All voiceover is clear, articulate English (or English-subtitled).
  - [ ] Video length: Under 120 seconds (2 minutes maximum).
- [ ] **The 5 Core Demonstration Moments Covered**:
  - [ ] 1. Loadout budget tradeoff and life-support meter dynamics (Power, Air, Health, Food).
  - [ ] 2. Space weather emergency with real DONKI solar flare data (Active Region AR3664).
  - [ ] 3. Mars communication latency warning (3–22 min speed-of-light delay).
  - [ ] 4. Data Loupe (`L` key) displaying **REAL DATA** vs **GAME RULE** chips.
  - [ ] 5. NASA Data Passport or Judge Mode (`?judge=1`) showing verified scientific sources.
- [ ] **Platform Upload (YouTube / Vimeo)**:
  - [ ] Video visibility set to **Public** or **Unlisted** (NEVER "Private").
  - [ ] Video title: `Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer - NASA Space Apps 2026 Demo`.
  - [ ] English subtitles uploaded from `docs/submission/subtitles.srt` or `subtitles.vtt`.
  - [ ] Link verified in a private/incognito window without requiring login or age-gate.
